## A short reflection

The real change in September was not on any list: bedtime moved to half past eight and held. That is roughly two hours a night, and most of them went to books, partly by choice and partly because the sofa is too close to the girls’ door for anything with a soundtrack. The abandoned book bothers me less than it used to. October has a concert in it, so the balance will tip back towards the screen and the stage.
