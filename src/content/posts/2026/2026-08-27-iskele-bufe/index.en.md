A standing-room counter by the Karaköy pier. The mackerel sandwich is good, there is nowhere to sit, and the pickle juice is the best thing they sell.

## Facts

- Went with | Alone, between two meetings
- Booking | None, you queue at the counter

## What we ordered

Three things, all of them eaten standing up.

- **Mackerel sandwich with extra onion**: Half a loaf, fish straight off the grill, more onion than I asked for. The bread gives up at the last third, so eat fast.
- **Pickle juice**: A cold plastic cup of it, sharp and salty, with two slices of pickled cabbage at the bottom. The best thing on the counter, and I went back for a second.
- **Mixed pickles**: Cucumber, carrot and one hot pepper I did not finish. Fine, but the juice already does this job.

Four minutes from ordering to the first bite. Nobody asks how it was, and nobody needs to.

## The verdict

İskele Büfe is lunch as a transaction, and it is honest about that. The sandwich is better than it has to be this close to a ferry pier: the fish is grilled to order and the onion is cut that morning, not the night before. What costs it the last star and a half is everything around the sandwich. There is one narrow shelf along the wall and no stool, so I ate with a laptop bag between my feet and a napkin in each hand. With twenty minutes between two meetings that suits me. With the twins and a pram it would not work at all.

## Would I go again?

Yes, alone and in a hurry, and mostly for the pickle juice.
