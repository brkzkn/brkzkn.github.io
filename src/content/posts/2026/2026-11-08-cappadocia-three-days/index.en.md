Three days in Göreme with two two-year-olds. We did not fly in a balloon, and I do not think we missed anything.

## Summary

We watched the balloons from the terrace at 06:40 with tea instead of flying. One valley walk a day at toddler speed, pottery in Avanos, testi kebab once; the full itinerary and notes are inside.

## Day by day

Times are rough. One walk a day, planned around the nap.

### 2026-11-06 | Fly in, settle, one short walk

- 09:10 | Sabiha Gökçen to Kayseri. Two seats, two laps, and the pram handed over at the gate.
- 11:00 | Rental car at Kayseri with two car seats booked ahead. About an hour to Göreme; both asleep before we left the city.
- 15:30 | After the nap, the flat start of Pigeon Valley from the Göreme end. Forty minutes out, forty back, most of it spent collecting stones.
- 18:30 | Testi kebab at [restaurant name]. They crack the clay pot open at the table, which was the only part the girls cared about.

## Practical notes

- Ask the hotel the evening before whether the balloons will fly. Wind cancels them and nobody knows until late.
- The terrace is cold at 06:40 in November. Coats, a flask of tea and something to eat bought us twenty-five minutes.
- A cave room stays dark and quiet, so both naps ran long. Plan the afternoon walk for 15:30, not 14:00.
- The valleys stop being pram country after the first few hundred metres. One in the carrier, one on foot, then swap.
- Opening hours and ticket prices for the museum sites change with the season: [check before going].

## Favourite moments

The second morning on the terrace: one of them pointing at each balloon in turn and saying "more", the other more interested in the sugar cubes. The clay pot being cracked open at the table. And a stretch of Pigeon Valley where nobody was in a hurry and a pocket full of stones counted as the whole plan.

## What I would change next time

- Fly into Nevşehir if the times work. It is closer than Kayseri and saves most of the drive.
- Stay four nights. With two mornings, one cancelled balloon day leaves a single chance.
- One stop after Avanos, not two. One car nap is useful; a second one costs the afternoon.
