A lunch-only lokanta in Acıbadem with a garden and six pots a day. We went on a Saturday with the twins in high chairs, and nobody minded the noise.

## Facts

- Went with | Two adults, two two-year-olds
- Booking | None taken, tables go from noon

## What we ordered

Four of the six pots, shared across the table.

- **Aubergine stew with lamb**: The pot to come for. Soft aubergine, very little oil, lamb that needed no knife.
- **White beans**: Plain and properly cooked. The girls ate most of the plate between them.
- **Rice with orzo**: Buttery, each grain separate. Went with everything, which is its job.
- **Cacık**: Cold, thin, a lot of mint. Right for a July lunch in the shade.
- **Semolina helva**: One portion, four spoons. Warm, with pine nuts, gone in two minutes.

Two high chairs were at the table before we asked, and the food arrived about five minutes after we pointed at the counter.

## The verdict

There is no menu. You walk past the counter, look into the pots and point, and by half past two most of them are empty. The garden has eight tables under a fig tree and a mulberry, and the gravel kept the twins busy for longer than any toy we had packed.

I am not giving it a rating yet. One lunch means four pots out of six on one Saturday, and a kitchen that cooks something different every day deserves more than one visit before it gets a number.

## Would I go again?

Yes, and soon, on a weekday, to see what the other pots are like.
