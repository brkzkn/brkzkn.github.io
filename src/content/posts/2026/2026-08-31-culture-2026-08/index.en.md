## A short reflection

August had one film, no concerts and a series I could not be bothered to finish, and I do not think it was a worse month for it. The evenings were long, the balcony tomatoes ripened, and the one book I read got my full attention for four weeks. September will bring the screens back. I would like to keep the habit of one long book at a time.
