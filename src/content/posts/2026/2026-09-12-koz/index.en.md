Twelve seats around a charcoal grill in Yeldeğirmeni. The lamb ribs came off the fire four at a time, and nobody tried to hurry us out.

## What we ordered

Six things for three people, in the order they reached us.

- **Lamb ribs**: The reason for the five stars. Four at a time, straight from the grill to the plate, fat rendered until the edges crackled and the meat still pink at the bone. We ordered a second round before finishing the first.
- **Adana kebab**: Hand-chopped, not minced, and you can tell. Hot enough to notice, not hot enough to need the ayran.
- **Liver skewers**: Small cubes, ninety seconds a side. Soft in the middle, served with cumin and raw onion. The friend who does not eat liver ate three.
- **Ezme**: Chopped to order on a board in front of us. Sharp with pomegranate molasses, and better than it needed to be for a side.
- **Grilled onion salad with sumac**: Charred whole in the embers, then peeled and dressed. Sweet, smoky and gone in two minutes.
- **Lavash**: Warmed on the grill and pressed over the meat to catch the fat. They kept bringing it without being asked.

One man works the grill and one works the room, and each plate arrived just as the last one was cleared. We sat for two and a half hours and never once looked for a waiter.

## The verdict

Köz is two streets behind the Yeldeğirmeni mural walls, a single room with a copper hood, a long marble counter and twelve stools. There is no table to hide at. You sit an arm’s length from the charcoal, you watch your own skewers turn, and the usta hands the plate across the counter himself. That is most of the appeal, and it is also why the food is so good: nothing waits under a lamp, and nothing travels further than a metre.

I give five stars rarely, so here are the reasons. The ribs are the best I have eaten on this side of the city, and they were just as good on the second round as on the first. The pace was right: fast when we were hungry, slow once we were not. And the bill for three came to less than I expected to pay for two, with exactly what we ate on it: no cover charge, no line for bread, nothing added at the end.

The room is loud and smoky, and my jacket smelled of charcoal until Monday. I would not change either.

## Would I go again?

Yes, and soon. Same stools, ribs first, and with no plans for afterwards.
