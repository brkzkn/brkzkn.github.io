Out on the first sea bus, back on the last one we could manage. A whole city in thirteen and a half hours, with two people who nap when they choose to.

## Summary

The early sea bus to Mudanya, İskender for lunch, the covered bazaar and tea in Koza Han. We skipped the Uludağ cable car because of the queue and were home by nine with two sleeping girls.

## Facts

- Getting there | Sea bus, Kadıköy to Mudanya, then a taxi
- Travelling | Two adults, twins at two years and one month

## Day by day

Times are rough. The day bent around one nap, which happened in a taxi.

### 2026-08-22 | Sea bus, İskender, bazaar

- 07:30 | Sea bus from Kadıköy. Seats by the window, breakfast from a bag, both of them awake since six and pleased about it.
- 09:30 | Mudanya pier. A second tea on the waterfront, then a taxi up to the city. They slept for the whole ride, which was the plan.
- 10:45 | The Grand Mosque. Shoes off, twenty domes, and a fountain in the middle of the hall that they would have stood beside until evening.
- 12:30 | İskender at [place name], one and a half portions between four. The butter is poured at the table and the girls ate mostly the bread underneath.
- 14:00 | The covered bazaar, slowly, then Koza Han. Tea in the courtyard, one silk scarf bought, two declined.
- 16:15 | The cable car station for Uludağ. The queue was past the car park, so we looked at the cabins going up and turned around.
- 18:30 | Sea bus back from Mudanya. One asleep before we left the pier, the other somewhere past the breakwater.
- 21:00 | Home. Two transfers from pram to bed, both successful.

## Favourite moments

The fountain inside the Grand Mosque, with daylight coming through the glass dome above it and two small people whispering because everyone else was. The courtyard of Koza Han at three in the afternoon, when the shade reached our table. And the evening sea bus, the first forty minutes of quiet either of us had had all day.

## What I would change next time

- Cable car first, at opening time, then the city.
- Stay one night. Thirteen hours out of the house is the limit at this age, and we found it.
- Order two full portions of İskender. One and a half was optimism.
